# Core Objective

Enable tea collectors to maintain awareness of their collection's state — what they have, what's aging, what needs attention — so they make informed purchase and brewing decisions.

The objective is not to teach users about tea or optimize their brewing. It's to close the information gap between what's physically in the cabinet and what the collector can recall about it. If the user opens the app and immediately knows which teas to prioritize today, the objective is met.
