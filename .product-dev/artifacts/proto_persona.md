**Name & Sketch**: Lena — a tea collector whose cabinet has outgrown her memory but not her standards for what she buys and from whom.

**Relevant Context**:
- Orders from 4-6 specialty vendors, mostly online, with seasonal buying spikes (spring harvests, autumn oolongs)
- Maintains ~30-45 teas across a dedicated shelf and overflow storage
- Has strong vendor preferences — will reorder from a specific farm if last year's harvest was good

**Goals**:
- Know what's on hand before placing another order — avoid duplicates, fill gaps
- Drink her teas while they're still worth drinking, especially greens and lightly oxidized oolongs
- Maintain the thread between a tea she enjoys and where it came from

**Frustrations**:
- Tried a spreadsheet, abandoned it after three weeks — too much friction for something that should feel like a hobby, not bookkeeping
- Found a bag of aged white tea she forgot she had — good surprise, but also found a stale sencha she should've finished months ago
- When a vendor's seasonal catalog drops, she can't quickly check whether she still has last year's version

**Behaviors**:
- Opens the cabinet and visually scans before deciding what to brew — "shopping" her own collection
- Takes a photo of new arrivals but never organizes them into anything searchable
- Bookmarks vendor pages in browser tabs that pile up and eventually get lost

**Quote**: "I know what I like, I just can't always remember what I have."
