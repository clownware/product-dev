**Primary Questions**:

1. Can users add a new tea to their collection in under 30 seconds?
   - **Signal**: Task completion time; whether users hesitate at the vendor field or type selector
   - **Method**: Timed task observation — "You just received this tea, add it to your collection"

2. Do users notice and act on freshness indicators without being prompted?
   - **Signal**: Unprompted tap on a yellow/red-flagged tea during free exploration
   - **Method**: Observation during open-ended task — "Take a look at your collection and do whatever feels natural"

3. Do users reference vendor information when making a reorder decision?
   - **Signal**: User mentions vendor name or taps through to vendor detail when asked "You're running low on oolong, what would you do?"
   - **Method**: Scenario task with think-aloud

**Secondary Questions**:

1. Where do users hesitate or get confused in the add-tea flow?
   - **Signal**: Pauses longer than 3 seconds, wrong taps, verbal confusion ("what goes here?")
   - **Method**: Think-aloud observation + post-task debrief

2. How do users scan the collection overview — by freshness, by type, or by recency?
   - **Signal**: Eye movement pattern and first tap during free browse
   - **Method**: Observation during open exploration, followed by "What were you looking for?"
