**Entry Point**: Lena receives a tea delivery and opens the app to log her new purchase before it disappears into the cabinet.

**Flow Steps**:

1. **Action**: Opens app from home screen
   **Sees**: Collection overview — grid of teas sorted by attention needed (freshness alerts at top, recently added below)
   **Decides**: Taps "Add tea"

2. **Action**: Types tea name and selects type (green, oolong, pu-erh, etc.)
   **Sees**: Simple form with name, type, and vendor fields. Vendor field suggests from previously entered vendors.
   **Decides**: Selects existing vendor or types a new one

3. **Action**: Sets quantity and marks as sealed or opened (defaults to sealed, today's date)
   **Sees**: Preview card showing how this tea will appear in the collection
   **Decides**: Taps "Save"

4. **Action**: Returns to collection overview
   **Sees**: New tea appears in the grid. Notices an older tea flagged yellow — "opened 3 weeks ago, brew soon"
   **Decides**: Taps the flagged tea to investigate

5. **Action**: Views tea detail
   **Sees**: Tea name, vendor name, days since opened, estimated freshness window remaining, quantity left
   **Decides**: Brews a cup, taps "Brewed" to decrement quantity

6. **Action**: Returns to overview
   **Sees**: Updated collection — flagged tea now shows reduced quantity, freshness status unchanged
   **Decides**: Done — closes app

**Exit Point**: Lena's collection reflects the new arrival and the consumed serving. She knows what she has and what needs drinking.

**Critical Moment**: Step 2 — entering the tea details. If the form requires too many fields or doesn't remember vendors, the friction breaks the habit. This is where "less effort than a spreadsheet" lives or dies.
